App Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Your privacy matters to us. The Pawan Hot Chips app does not access personal or sensitive data stored on your Android or iOS device. This policy explains what that means and what happens when you choose to contact us through another service.

Information and device permissions

The app does not request access to your contacts, location, photos, videos, camera, microphone, files, messages, or call history. It does not collect, store, or transmit information from those areas of your device.

Information you choose to share

If you contact us by phone, email, or WhatsApp, you may choose to share details such as your name, phone number, or order enquiry. Those details are sent through the communication service you choose and may be used by us to respond to your enquiry. Please avoid sending payment card details or other sensitive information in a message.

Third-party services

The app or this website may provide links to services operated by others, such as WhatsApp, phone, email, or map providers. If you follow one of those links, that provider may collect or process information under its own privacy policy. We do not control those services or their data practices.

Data security and retention

Because the app does not access personal data on your device, it has no device-derived personal data to delete. Information you choose to send through a third-party communication service is subject to that service's practices as well as how we handle your enquiry.

Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has shared personal information with us through a direct message, please contact us so we can review the request.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the app's features or data practices change. The latest version and its update date will be published on this page.
